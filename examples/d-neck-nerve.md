# Example D (man, neck nerve pain)

Profile: man with a neck nerve problem that makes lateral raises painful. Y raises do not bother it. 4 letters.

Plan:
- A: Dumbbell Weighted Situps, Weighted Planks, Low Incline Dumbbell Bench Press, DB Floor Fly, High Incline Dumbbell Bench Press, Incline DB Y Raise
- B: Dumbbell Chest Supported Row, Lat Pulldown, Single Arm Dumbbell Row, Dumbbell Kelso Shrug
- C: Dumbbell Alternating Hammer Curl, Seated Underhand Grip Bicep Curl, Incline EZ Bar Skullcrushers, Single Leg Calf Raises, Side Lying External Rotations
- D: Front Loaded Barbell Reverse Lunge, Nordic Ham Curl, Sissy Squat, Barbell Hip Thrusts

Why:
- Letter A looks like it has three chest movements, but a high incline press is not really a chest movement. It is a vertical press ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Chest).
- A Y raise replaces the lateral raise, because lateral raises are painful with a neck nerve problem and Y raises are not ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Shoulders).
- Nordic ham curls are not an ideal movement. They are used only when there is no machine, glute ham raise or ball, and only for someone already really strong, who does the plan consistently and carries little excess weight ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Glutes and hamstrings).
- On the leg letter the sissy squat is last ([section 11](../programming/11-order-inside-a-workout.md)).
