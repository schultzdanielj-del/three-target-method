# Example A (coach)

Profile: man, adjustable dumbbells up to 90 lb, narrow hip structure and narrow waist. 4 letters.

Plan:
- A: Roman Chair Side Hold, Roman Chair Situps, Low Incline DB Bench, Decline DB Bench, Seated DB Shoulder Press, Seated DB Lateral Raises
- B: Chest Supported DB Rows, Pulldowns, Single Arm DB Rows, Reverse Hypers
- C: Single Leg Calf Raises, Alternating DB Hammer Curl, Standing DB Curls, Lying DB Triceps Extensions, Straight Bar Pushdown
- D: Front Loaded Barbell Reverse Lunges, Glute Ham Raises, Dumbbell Sissy Squat, Barbell Hip Thrusts

Why:
- Decline DB bench is the second chest movement instead of a fly, because a fly is easy to cheat ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Chest; [section 11](../programming/11-order-inside-a-workout.md)).
- Roman chair side holds are in this plan because of a very narrow hip structure and narrow waist: added oblique muscle gives a good shape. That is not the case for every individual ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Abs).
- The seated DB press comes after the bench presses. Never vertical press before horizontal, it feels terrible. Horizontal then vertical is fine ([section 11](../programming/11-order-inside-a-workout.md)).
- Reverse hypers are last on the back letter. Hinges go near the end, so the spine and core are still fresh and less weight is needed ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Hinges).
- Calves sit on an upper body letter. You cannot train calves first and then do lunges and squats on cooked calves ([section 10](../programming/10-calves.md)).
- On the leg letter the lunges are first and the sissy squat is last. The knees need to be warmed up, and being pre-fatigued means less load, so the knees do not blow up ([section 11](../programming/11-order-inside-a-workout.md)).
- The leg letter has 4 exercises, because legs are more taxing on the body ([section 2](../programming/02-letters-and-schedule.md)).
