# Example B (woman, glute and hamstring dominant goals)

Profile: woman, experienced lifter, female physique goals (more emphasis on lower body, shoulders and back). Glute and hamstring dominant goals. 4 letters.

Plan:
- A: Bulgarian Split Squat, Pendulum Squat, Captains Chair Knee Raise, Plank
- B: High Incline Dumbbell Bench Press, Assisted Dip Machine, Incline Dumbbell Tricep Extension, Standing Dumbbell Lateral Raise, Incline Dumbbell Y Raise
- C: Machine Seated Leg Curl, 45 Degree Back Raise, Barbell Hip Thrust, Machine Hip Abduction, Toe Press
- D: Assisted Chinup Machine, Dumbbell Single Arm Row, Seated Cable Row, Rope Facepull, Seated Dumbbell Curl

Why:
- The split is not push, pull, legs. The letters carry more lower body, shoulders and back. Legs are spread over two letters (A and C) and the arm work is folded into the push and pull letters ([section 2](../programming/02-letters-and-schedule.md)).
- Glutes, lateral hip and hamstrings are split across letters so they are hit fresher and harder ([section 7](../programming/07-movements-per-muscle.md)).
- Letter A has two quad movements: one single leg movement and one deep knee bend movement ([section 7](../programming/07-movements-per-muscle.md), [section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md) Quads).
- Letter C has four glute and hamstring movements. Glutes and hamstrings are two separate muscle groups, with 2 direct movements each, and the goals are glute and hamstring dominant, so the maximum slots are filled ([section 7](../programming/07-movements-per-muscle.md)).
- Calves (the toe press) sit on a leg letter here, because the legs are split across two days, so calves can go on one of them ([section 10](../programming/10-calves.md)).
- The high incline press fills the vertical press slot ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Shoulders).
- The Y raise is the second shoulder movement. It is a cheap way to get some lateral delt work while building shoulder mobility and scapular stability ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Shoulders).
- Dips count as chest and triceps, and can count as the second triceps movement. People start on the assisted dip machine ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Biceps and triceps).
- The back letter has a face pull and no rear delt fly. In Dan's coaching experience, people new to the method nearly always have weak upper backs and need a good chunk of time on external rotations, lower traps, serratus and scapular tilt movements. The rear delt fly is a heavy duty movement. The plan is to top out the face pull (can no longer stand anchored against the weight), beat it as a confidence booster, then upgrade to the rear delt fly ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Back).
