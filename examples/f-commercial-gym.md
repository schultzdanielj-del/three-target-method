# Example F (woman, commercial gym, shoulder mechanics)

Profile: woman who trains in a commercial gym. Shoulder issues: poor scapular stability, no low traps, no scapular tilt, serratus or first rib mobility. 4 letters. Four slots have a second option for the same muscle (shown as the first option below): Incline Push Ups for the low incline press, Bent Over Rear Delt Fly for the Reverse Pec Deck, Captains Chair Knee Raise for the situp, Romanian Deadlift for the back raise.

Plan:
- A: Calf Raise Machine, Machine Chest Press, Low Incline DB Bench, Dumbbell Lateral Raises, Side Lying External Rotations
- B: Assisted Neutral Grip Pullups, Hammer Strength Chest Supported Row, Lat Pulldowns, Single Arm DB Rows, Reverse Pec Deck
- C: Decline Bench Situps, Side Plank, Incline DB Triceps Extensions, Straight Bar Pushdowns, Seated Dumbbell Curls
- D: Bulgarian Split Squats, Leg Press, 45 Degree Back Raises, Machine Seated Leg Curls

Why:
- The second options on four slots are because the machines and benches may be taken at busy times (introduction of [programming.md](../programming/00-intro.md)).
- Calves are first on the letter so they are not skipped ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Other).
- Two chest presses, lateral raises and side lying external rotations, with no vertical press, because of the shoulder issues: poor scapular stability, no low traps, no tilt ability or serratus, and a badly restricted first rib. In Dan's coaching experience, 80% or more of people have these shoulder problems ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Shoulders; [section 23](../programming/23-upper-body-and-hips-principles.md)).
- The side lying external rotation is a remedial exercise that can be added instead of a second shoulder slot when someone has general shoulder issues ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Shoulders).
