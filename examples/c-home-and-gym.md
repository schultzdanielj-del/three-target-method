# Example C (woman, trains at home and at a commercial gym)

Profile: woman with a home setup and access to a commercial gym. Poor overhead mobility (first rib and thoracic spine restrictions). Experienced lifter. 4 letters. Home option shown below. Every slot has a second, gym option for the same muscle (for example Machine Chest Press, Pec Deck, Assisted Chinup Machine, Chest Supported Row Machine, Reverse Pec Deck, Machine Seated Leg Curl).

Plan (home option):
- A: Low Incline Dumbbell Bench Press, High Incline Dumbbell Bench Press, Seated Lateral Raises, Standing Lateral Raises, Single Leg Calf Raises
- B: Banded Pullups, Inverted Rows, Barbell Rows, Head Supported Rear Delt Flyes, Chest Supported Dumbbell Rows
- C: Ab Wheel Rollouts, Side Planks, Alternating Dumbbell Curls, Barbell Curls, Barbell Skullcrusher, Incline Dumbbell Triceps Extensions
- D: ATG Split Squats, Ball Hamstring Curls, Romanian Deadlifts, Barbell Hip Thrust

Why:
- Each slot has a home option and a commercial gym option, with the plan toggling between them (introduction of [programming.md](../programming/00-intro.md)).
- Two lateral raise movements and a high incline press, with no overhead press, because of the poor overhead mobility ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Shoulders; [section 17](../programming/17-mobility-and-body-type.md)).
- Barbell Rows are in the home version because of an equipment limitation. A barbell row near the end of the workout is acceptable when there is no other row option and the person is not yet strong enough to run into problems ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Other).
- A Romanian deadlift is here despite the usual leaving out of Romanian deadlifts, because she is a lifter whose technique is well established and known, and it is a lighter, fixed reps and sets movement ([section 8](../programming/08-choosing-the-movement.md), Hinges; [section 17](../programming/17-mobility-and-body-type.md)).
- The leg letter has 4 exercises, because legs are more taxing on the body ([section 2](../programming/02-letters-and-schedule.md)).
