# Suspension trainer (TRX)

A suspension trainer is a better choice if the person does not have access to equipment. With only a few dumbbells or bands, the ability to gradate progress and improve incrementally is stuck. A suspension trainer can be gradated inch by inch.
- Keep track of two positions: where the feet are (or the hands, in some small circumstances), and how long the strap is. Both are different for each exercise.
- If the person accurately tracks the strap length and the position of their body on the floor in relation to where the strap is anchored, for each exercise, they can track progress very accurately and set PRs incrementally. This is true for all suspension training movements.
- All of these movements should have a slight pause at end range, just to demonstrate a lack of momentum. Do not pause longer than needed and make it harder than it needs to be.
- The strap can rub against the forearms uncomfortably. Wear a long sleeve shirt.

**TRX row.** The exact same scapula mechanics as the chest supported dumbbell row or any rowing motion, with the added need to keep a straight, locked body from head to toe. So it takes a bit of plank mechanics and core strength.

**TRX chest press.** Similar to a pushup, with the same plank mechanics.
- Active scapular depression the entire time. The lats and low traps must fire to do it. If they do not, the shoulders roll up into the ears and the scapula collapse, and the person cannot use the chest muscles effectively or spread the load across the shoulder complex safely.
- Because the straps float freely, they wobble more, so the person needs a stronger grip and even more scapular stability.
- The scapula freely retract and protract during the motion.
- At the bottom the shoulders should not roll forward. If the scapula are effectively depressed, this will not happen, and the pecs get loaded.

**TRX fallout.** An ab wheel rollout in suspension trainer mode, with the same plank mechanics. The hips do not need to sit back. They can stay extended the entire time.
