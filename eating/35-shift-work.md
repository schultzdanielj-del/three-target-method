# 35. Shift work

Mode 1 starts when they wake up. Mode 2 starts at whatever time equates to similar timing. Shift workers benefit from 1 mg of melatonin before bed more than most people would (Dan's recollection from years ago that there is some evidence for shift work and not for normal schedules, not a study he checked; see [section 40](40-evidence-and-its-limits.md) for what is checked). Where the repo is silent, the AI says so. It does not invent routine rules such as "keep it the same most days" or set clock times.
