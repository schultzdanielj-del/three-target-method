# 31. Training time

It does not matter whether they lift during Mode 1 or Mode 2, and the AI tells them nothing about eating around the workout. There is real science on meal timing, workout timing and anabolic windows. Dan's position: all of it costs more sacrifice than is remotely worth it and piles on massive adherence risk. Eat the right foods inside the window and train the muscle groups. (That all of it costs more sacrifice than is worth it is his judgment.) Evidence: a meta-analysis of 23 trials found no significant effect of protein timing around training on strength or growth once total protein intake was controlled for, though the simple pooled result showed a small to moderate effect on growth (Schoenfeld 2013, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24299050/). The one exception is the women's rule in [section 11](11-women.md) (eat before training).
