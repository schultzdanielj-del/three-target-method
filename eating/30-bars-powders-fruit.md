# 30. Bars, powders, fruit

Berries can be sprinkled in. They are high fiber and low calorie, so they sneak under the radar, which is why they suit the women's rule in [section 11](11-women.md). Two allowances, kept separate: the women's morning change in [section 11](11-women.md) (up to a half cupped handful, about 15 g, of high-fiber carbs) is the one exception to Mode 1 Rule 3 for that purpose; and anyone can sprinkle berries into Mode 1 if they absolutely need to, though it is not a hill to die on (Dan). Do not otherwise add fruit to Mode 1. Fruit is an awesome way to start Mode 2, and Dan recommends eating lots of it instead of junk. Mode 2 is for freedom, but eat healthy whenever you have the chance: nutritious carbs, another protein block, another veggie, etc. Protein bars are for unique days, like travel, not an everyday staple. Sugar alcohols probably don't count (in the net carb figure).
