# 3. Why it works (the AI explains this)

It helps to understand why the style works. Without that, people follow rules by rote, and when the rules get hard they drop everything because they have no basis for a contextual decision. The idea: keep unstructured eating for the parts of life where other values are in play, like family or social events. During work hours, or whenever it is just you eating, eat the Mode 1 foods. That puts a very large part of the day in a caloric deficit while checking off the mandatory food types. There is almost always a solution for whatever kind of day someone is having that gets them very close to or on the mark. There may be adjustments here and there. The goal is a very large part of the day in a deficit while eating the mandatory foods, then finding the moments that would take a much greater sacrifice and using the wiggle room from the deficit to fill that gap.

The structure puts the strict part in a chunk of the day when most people are working and busy, and saves the calories for the time of day when it is hardest to be perfect anyway (Dan).

The floor and the swings (Dan). The floor is so high that the damage from a bad week is barely anything, and in a good week you make strong progress: drawdowns are capped and breakouts have a tailwind. You still have to pilot yourself in Mode 2, but you can live your life, work around things and have some freedom. Some nights you might have salmon, rice and vegetables and go to bed with a large deficit. Other nights it is wings and trivia at the bar. The aggregate is a deficit over time with bare minimum sacrifice. You are not wildly hungry all the time, and you are not saying no to every temptation 24 hours a day. No study tests this aggregate pattern.

The hybrid car metaphor. It does not translate directly into the science of how it works, but it is a good way to view the structure. The body is a hybrid car that runs on gas or electricity. Gas is carbohydrates, glycogen or blood sugar. Electricity is body fat stores. When you are cruising around, you should be in electric mode all the time. When you do hard activity, you burn gas. If you keep filling the tank with gas, there is too much gas in the tank and the body struggles to switch to electric mode. The goal of the eating style is to fill the gas tank only as much as it needs to be filled to do your lifting, filled by the unavoidable moments of life that conflict with other values. The rest of your life is electric mode: a big caloric deficit, with the difference filled by tapping body fat stores, while you eat the mandatory foods for health and body composition.
