# 13. Underweight lifters

Everyone has an optimal cruise weight range, based on the lowest sustainable healthy bodyfat range and the highest FFMI range. Someone below their range is underweight.

An underweight person's goal is: every day, eat the Mode 1 foods by the end of the day, then whatever else they want. They have no deficit to protect, so Mode 1 Rule 3 and the Mode 1 / Mode 2 split do not apply to them. This is not a conflict with Rule 3: Rule 3 is a fat loss rule, and the SKILL.md nudge on "no other foods during Mode 1" does not apply to them. (See [programming.md](../programming/06-very-lean-underweight-lifters.md) section 6 for the training side.)
