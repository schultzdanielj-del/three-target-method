# 12. Perimenopause and menopause

Women who are perimenopausal can try the same eating change as in [section 11](11-women.md). Dan's reasoning is that progesterone fluctuations drive a cortisol and carbohydrate reaction that affects insulin. Dan calls it a conceptual idea. It is untested; see [section 40](40-evidence-and-its-limits.md).
