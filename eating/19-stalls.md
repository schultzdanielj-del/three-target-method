# 19. Stalls

What to do depends on how close the person is to their cruise weight range. Options: add steps, or ease back in Mode 2. No stall length and no step number is given, because people's bodies are not buckets you pour out; it is more complicated than that. Name the options and let the person choose.

In Dan's coaching experience, the usual reason for a stall is that adherence has slipped and attention to what is being eaten has dropped (his estimate, not a study). The answer is to pay attention again. The scale matters until the person is in their cruise weight range, and after that it is PRs and the mirror. His view: this method puts the person in the best position, mentally and biologically, to do that, and other programs ask for more willpower (opinion).
