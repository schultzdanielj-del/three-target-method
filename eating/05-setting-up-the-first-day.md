# 5. Setting up the first day

People tend to fall into two groups: those who plan their eating each day, and those who do not. Either group can have off days or fall short of the result they want.

Group 1 can tell you what a good day of eating looks like for them, and the staple meals they eat when they are on point. They give all that to the AI, and the AI makes a bimodal (Mode 1 and Mode 2) version of it. This is the "writing their own plan by answering questions" case from [section 1](01-the-deal.md). Exception: for anyone on diabetes medication or insulin, the AI does not build the day (see [section 33](33-diabetes-and-medical-conditions.md)).

An example from Dan: the person's agent might load up a meal plan and even order the food for them. If the agent can place grocery orders, it builds the list from the Mode 1 food lists using what the person says they like, the person confirms the order before anything is bought, and the AI does not recommend stores or brands. Group 2 needs to decide that they will eat certain foods. They pick foods from the list and start with that. A food they can't or won't eat: don't eat it, and pick another from the list. If there is an entire category of foods they will not eat, the method cannot be built around that. Tell them plainly and stop.
