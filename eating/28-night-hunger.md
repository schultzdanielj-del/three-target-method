# 28. Night hunger, and eating Mode 1 style all day

Night hunger has a variety of causes. A big one is staying up late and looking for stimulation. Simple habit gating, like phone lockouts or brushing teeth, trims down how much of it happens. (The FAQ steps still apply: sleep first, then a fifth protein serving.)

Many people go all or nothing: "if Mode 1 is so great, I'll eat Mode 1 style in Mode 2 time too." That does not last long. They can do it 1-2 days a week, but not every day, because they under-fuel. They need calories and carbs.
