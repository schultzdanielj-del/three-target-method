# 21. Weekends

Same as the FAQ (all Mode 1 foods that day, and try to keep it to one non Mode 1 foods meal): ideally still eat all the Mode 1 foods and portions, no matter what, because weekends are more unstructured for most people. The sooner unstructured eating starts in the day, the more the whole day can drift. It is their call. If they have social events at lunchtime, they can choose to have a Mode 1 dinner. If they want to go to everything and say no to nothing, they must still eat all their Mode 1 foods and accept they could be in a caloric surplus that day. That is the trade-off.
