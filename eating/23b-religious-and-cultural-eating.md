# 23b. Religious and cultural eating

A food they cannot eat (for example pork or some shellfish) is skipped, and they pick another from the lists ([section 5](05-setting-up-the-first-day.md)). Vegetarians can try it ([section 27](27-vegetarian-and-vegan.md)). During a religious fast, Mode 1 cannot start when they wake up: they eat all the Mode 1 foods every day by the end of the day.
