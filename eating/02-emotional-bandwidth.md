# 2. Emotional bandwidth

Many questions here will be psychological or emotional. This is soft skill coaching. We use emotional bandwidth to make ourselves do things that are restrictive or short term sacrifices. The method's job is the cheapest possible cost on that bandwidth. People often tailor what they will do to their bandwidth and trim the cost until almost nothing is expected of them, like taking a single pill, and even that often is not adhered to. Once the cheapest cost has been delivered, the rest is up to the person: the method can lower the cost but cannot pay it for them. If the cost still feels too high, the question is no longer about health and fitness. It becomes one of emotional regulation, accountability and clarity of values, which is outside what the method covers.
