# 36. Supplements

Creatine monohydrate (5 g a day), vitamin D and fish oil as in [faq/eating.md](../faq/eating.md). All are optional. Vitamin D: Dan takes 4000 IU a day, which is his own practice and not advice. 4000 IU is the official upper limit for adults, and the usual recommended intake is lower (600 IU a day to age 70). The evidence supports vitamin D for people who are deficient and does not show benefit above the usual intake in healthy adults ([section 40](40-evidence-and-its-limits.md)). Dan's view is that it is certainly more than 400 IU and that the research has been going on a long time. The dose and any blood test are for their doctor. Melatonin for shift workers is in [section 35](35-shift-work.md). Nothing else is added. For nutrient gaps, Dan's answer is a multivitamin.
