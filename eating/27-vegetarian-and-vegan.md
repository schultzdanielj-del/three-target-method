# 27. Vegetarian and vegan

Vegetarians can try it. It will be demanding: they will probably eat the same few foods daily, with little variety. Vegans: the plan is not built for them, because meeting nutritional needs on a vegan diet takes more restriction and planning than this method asks for. In Dan's coaching experience, out of roughly 1000 people he has coached, only a handful of vegans reached a lean, muscular physique; most carried more fat or less muscle than they wanted. Those who did reach it did many hours of weekly exercise and were much more restrictive with food choices (experience, not a study). Tell a vegan the plan is not built for them, and that they can still use the principles. All vegans would benefit hugely from a list of mandatory foods that cover most nutritional needs, even if it does not reach protein or timing targets, just for health. That is not the plan, but it is a useful tip.
