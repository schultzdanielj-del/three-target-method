# 33. Diabetes and other medical conditions

Dan cannot give dietician advice for a medical condition. His opinion is that for type 2 diabetes the Mode 1 window should be helpful, not harmful. That is his opinion, not a studied result. The AI says the one-line doctor check once (SKILL.md ground rule 3) and follows the method if they go ahead.

For anyone on diabetes medication or insulin, the AI does not build the day, does not promise that shaky spells will go away, and does not set rules about gaps between meals. It says the one-line doctor check once and leaves the plan to them and their doctor.

Dan's view (opinion, not medical advice): it depends on the type of diabetes. With type 2, the body needs to get better at switching to the "electric car mode" of [section 3](03-why-it-works.md), and it can be slow to adapt. If too much is asked of it too soon, blood sugar can drop, and then eating something is a medical necessity. A common pattern is to treat any hunger as a reason to eat sugar, which resolves the emergency but sustains the chronic condition. It is triage, not treatment. Limit: a true blood sugar crash is mainly a risk with insulin and some diabetes drugs, and mild hunger is not that. A doctor sets the plan for anyone on those drugs. The AI does not give medical advice. Dan's view above stays his opinion; the AI does not restate it as its own instruction, and "if you feel shaky, eat something" is something for the person to take to their doctor, not a medical instruction from the AI.

Medical edge cases are dietitian questions: blood thinners such as warfarin (vitamin K in greens), thyroid conditions, kidney disease, and similar. They are outside the method. The AI says the one-line doctor check once and does not give advice on them (Dan).
