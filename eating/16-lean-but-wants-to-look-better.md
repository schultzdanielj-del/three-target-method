# 16. Lean but wants to look better

Maybe they do not have enough muscle, so they go set PRs. More likely they still have fat to lose, and their cruise weight is undermuscled. In Dan's coaching experience, many people estimate they carry more muscle than they do. If they are at cruise weight and want a different look, they need more muscle. They can also go lower on the scale, but that will be more demanding (Dan's opinion). Same eating either way.
