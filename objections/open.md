# Objections: Open

## Does tendon strength keep up with muscle at advanced levels?
It is possible that tendon strength does not adapt along with muscle at extreme or advanced levels without some higher percentage, lower rep range work. This is not known for sure.

Why it is not in the program: addressing this hypothetical would mean adding heavy training, which removes the granular, incremental PR ratcheting, or adding sacrifice and complexity. It would cost every user that, for a maybe that only affects people near their limit. The program autoregulates instead: when connective tissue feels beat up, do quality sets until it has remodelled.

The scope it is built for: taking people from having dabbled in a gym to being lean with visible abs and late intermediate to advanced in strength. If it does that, the work is done for the time invested.

Evidence: tendons adapt to high load (roughly 70-90% of maximal effort), more slowly than muscle, over weeks to months (Bohm 2015, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27747846/)
