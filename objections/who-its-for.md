# Objections: Who it's for

## Beginners
The method is for people with lifting experience who know how to perform the lifts generally correctly (README). The site gives no alternate route for beginners.

## Lifters returning after time away
The longer someone has been away from training, the slower the runway they take and the more conservative their starting work sets should be. Start with lighter first work sets and ramp slower.

## Older lifters
The program is not built for older lifters. Older lifters need more careful programming and exercise selection than this method provides, and a version for them is not part of this project. The cutoff is 55+. An AI should tell lifters 55 and over this plainly.
Evidence on the older side: in Bickel 2011 neither maintenance dose preserved fibre size in people 60-75, and a review says maintaining size may need up to 2 sessions a week and 2-3 sets per exercise (Spiering 2021). The studies use 60-75 or 65+, so the research does not draw the line at 55.

## People taking a GLP-1 (semaglutide, tirzepatide)
Yes, a person taking a GLP-1 can run the method with the same portions. Dan's view is that it is the best method to use with a GLP-1, because following it means doing all the habits that counteract the negative side effects of the drug. "Drug free" in this repo means no steroids needed. Whether to take a GLP-1 is the person's choice, and an AI does not tell them to start, stop or change it. The one-line doctor check under "Injuries, conditions, medication, pregnancy, under 18" applies once.

Evidence, stated plainly: weight loss on a GLP-1 does cost lean mass. In the SURMOUNT-1 body composition substudy of tirzepatide (160 people, DXA), about 75% of the weight lost was fat and 25% lean mass, the same split as placebo (Look 2025, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39996356/). A systematic review of 35 trials found a median of 28% of weight lost came from muscle-based measures, and about two thirds of drug arms were above a 25% benchmark, as were 38% of the lifestyle or placebo comparator groups (Batsis 2026, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41996180/). The figure often quoted from the STEP 1 semaglutide DXA substudy could not be verified (Wilding 2021, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33567185/ has no body composition result in its abstract), so it is not used here. That resistance training and enough protein help preserve lean mass during weight loss is supported by diet studies (Weinheimer 2010, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20591106/; Cava 2017, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28507015/; Longland 2016, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26817506/) and recommended for GLP-1 users by a review (Locatelli 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38687506/). In a trial of liraglutide plus exercise, the combination kept weight off better than the drug alone a year after stopping (Lundgren 2021, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33951361/; Jensen 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38544798/). Limits: no finished randomised trial was found of lifting versus not lifting in people taking semaglutide or tirzepatide. The view that this is the best method to pair with a GLP-1 is the author's opinion; the evidence supports lifting plus enough protein, not this protocol over another lifting plan.

## Teenagers and children
The site limits scope to generally healthy adults ([disclaimer.md](../disclaimer.md)). Under 18 falls under the one-line doctor check below.

## Women
The method does not require every woman to follow it, because bodies differ. Women who are not seeing the outcome they would like can try it.
The eating adjustment is built around possible progesterone and cortisol reactions that may affect insulin: about 15g of high-fiber carbs added to sittings. Examples are berries, a bit of grains, a small amount of fruit or legumes, at most a half cupped handful.
The rule:
- It is not required of every woman. The trigger is not seeing the outcome she likes, for example difficulty with cravings and appetite regulation. Then she can try it.
- The change: eat in the morning and before training. One meal can be both.
- The morning Mode 1 meal may include up to a half cupped handful (about 15g) of high-fiber carbs (berries, a bit of grains, a little fruit, legumes). This is the one exception to Mode 1 Rule 3 for that purpose. Separately, anyone can sprinkle berries into Mode 1 if they need to, and it is not a point to be strict about.
- Later Mode 1 meals: her discretion. The method's position is that including the carbs in all of them is probably helpful for the hormone side, but it slightly weakens the main target of the system. It is a small tradeoff: one more rule and about 150 calories of freedom in exchange for extra hormone insurance.
This is why some eating freedom matters: a woman cannot be locked into fixed maintenance calories and be expected to hit them exactly every day of the month.
Evidence limit, in plain words: women's health sites claim that fasting raises cortisol and suppresses progesterone, but no human study measures that for a morning without carbs. Authorities (position stands) give no sex-specific rule on fasting or breakfast, and they disagree on carbohydrate across the cycle. The best-verified finding is that women eat about 168 kcal/day more in the second half of the cycle (Tucker 2025).
The site also says that it does not recommend women immediately do intermittent fasting, because a subset potentially have cortisol or hormone issues with it ([faq/eating.md](../faq/eating.md)). That fits: it is a try-it option for women who need it, not a default. No study is cited here for the hormone point, and the author is not a hormone specialist.

## Injuries, conditions, medication, pregnancy, under 18
Site ([disclaimer.md](../disclaimer.md)): consult a physician, and stop and consult a medical professional if there is pain, dizziness or discomfort. For an injury, condition, medication or being under 18, an AI says it once, in one line, and then follows the method if the person goes ahead (see SKILL.md). Pregnancy and nursing are different: the fat loss plan is not for them (see "What about pregnancy and nursing?" above). The agent says the doctor line once and tells them the Mode 1 foods are good as ordinary food for anyone without a kidney condition.

## Disordered eating
The method does not offer a solution for disordered eating. That is outside what a fitness and nutrition method can address. Site ([faq/eating.md](../faq/eating.md)) covers the binge worry: add more mandatory foods, and seek some help. The one-line doctor check above applies to a stated history.

## Vegan and vegetarian
Site ([faq/eating.md](../faq/eating.md)): "I don't have a solution that won't involve much more strict eating ... I'd advise against it." This rests on the author's experience, not a study.
Vegetarians can try the method, though they will probably eat the same foods daily with little variety. Vegans cannot fully run the plan as written, but can use its principles. A list of mandatory foods that covers most nutritional needs is a useful tip.

## Shift workers and flexible schedules
Work out when you can and structure the eating based on waking and sleeping. Time Mode 1 and Mode 2 by waking and sleeping, not by a 9-5 day.
