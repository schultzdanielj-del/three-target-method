# 10. Calves

Calves are optional and are usually 1 movement. Put them on an upper body letter, not on leg day. If the person's legs are split across two days, calves can go on one of the leg days. You cannot train calves first and then do lunges and squats on cooked calves, and putting them at the end of leg day is a little too tiring and mentally likely to be skipped.

A person who wants big calves can get two calf movements (for example a double leg calf raise and a seated calf raise), like the 2 movements per muscle used for other groups.
