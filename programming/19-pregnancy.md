# 19. Pregnancy

Dan is not qualified to train pregnant people, and he has no records or experience running TTM during pregnancy. In general, what a pregnant person does depends on her state of fitness before the pregnancy: the better the state of fitness she starts from, the more she can do. Typically she does as much as she can until it feels like she cannot do it. As the stomach gets bigger and the hips get looser, some lower body exercises and a lot of core exercises become off limits. She continues with what feels good, and can push hard without feeling awkward. She will not make huge progress while pregnant: the goal is maintenance, general health and general conditioning. Some of the rules for carrying a lot of excess weight (section 18) apply, because the stomach obstructs positions. Joint laxity also needs to be taken care of.
