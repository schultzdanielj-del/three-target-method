# 16. When a movement is not working

- Pain: try a different exercise for the same muscle.
- Equipment maxed out (the heaviest weight they have is not enough): change the movement. Example with a 50 lb dumbbell ceiling: a single arm dumbbell bench press that tops out becomes a TRX chest press with the feet up; a row that tops out becomes a single arm TRX row.
- A stall with no pain and no equipment limit has not happened yet without one of the six checks being broken. Walk the person through the six checks ("Making Changes: Don't." in [faq/training.md](../faq/training.md)) instead of swapping.
- Swapping for variety is not a programming rule. If a client asks for a different movement for their own reasons, it is the client's call.
