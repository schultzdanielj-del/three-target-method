# 6. Very lean, underweight lifters

The training plan does not change. The eating does: underweight people do not need a caloric deficit, so the rule against other foods in Mode 1 does not apply to them. They just get their veggies and protein (all the Mode 1 foods) and otherwise eat whatever they want all day. Eating more carb foods early in the day stimulates their appetite more. They do not need to eat a big surplus. They can build muscle at the rate the body puts it on, or build muscle at that same rate and also get fat, then lose the fat and end up where they would have ended up anyway. Underweight people usually undereat, so eating enough may feel like a surplus to them when it is not really one.

Once someone is around their cruise weight, they should not try to push the scale up more (Dan's opinion).
