# 5. Do not accommodate self imposed limits

One client had only a suspension trainer and a possible AC joint sprain, and he was consistent with the eating but not the lifting. His plan took out direct shoulder work, which did no harm, and could not train the lower back at all with only a suspension trainer. The plan was to use the suspension trainer for 6 to 8 weeks, save the lunch money and buy a single adjustable dumbbell. He never did it. This was accommodating a person's self imposed rules about what they will and will not do. Do not offer it to other people. It does not replace the equipment minimum in the intro.

Most people start with a goal and then negotiate themselves completely out of any chance of achieving it with decisions like this.
