## 1 Timing
Divide your day of eating into two general "modes", split generally around late afternoon/end of work day.

Mode 1 Mode 2

Wake-up ~3:30-5pm Sleep

## 2 Mode 1

Eat exactly this during the first mode:

4 palms protein Protein powder Plain Greek Yogurt 0-2% 3-4 Eggs * Protein Bars (<15g net carb) Tinned Tuna Tinned Salmon Salmon Any White Fish Chicken/Turkey Chicken/Turkey Dark Meat * Beef * Pork * Any Ground Meat * * Any protein portion that also comes with 10g of fats or more counts as a fat thumb as well
3 fists vegetables Broccoli * Kale * Spinach * Brussels Sprouts * Asparagus * Cauliflower * Cabbage * Carrots Bell Peppers Cucumbers * cruciferous. At least one vegetable serving per day must be cruciferous.
1-2 thumbs fats Nuts Avocado 80%+ Dark Chocolate Nut Butter Olive Oil Coconut Oil Avocado Oil Non Light Cheese

Rule 1 You must eat all of these portions daily.
Rule 2 Eat them in any order/combination you like.
Rule 3 Don't eat any other foods during this time period. ? Zero calorie beverages are fine, any low calorie seasonings or low cal creamer in coffee is fine. Anything that's about 30 cals or less is not a big deal

## 3 Mode 2

Do whatever you want within reason as long as you eat one more palm of protein Protein powder Plain Greek Yogurt 0-2% 3-4 Eggs * Protein Bars (<15g net carb) Tinned Tuna Tinned Salmon Salmon Any White Fish Chicken/Turkey Chicken/Turkey Dark Meat * Beef * Pork * Any Ground Meat * * Any protein portion that also comes with 10g of fats or more counts as a fat thumb as well .

You may also move one palm of protein from Mode 1 over to night time to curb appetite.
